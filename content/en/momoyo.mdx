This system was developed as a Kerja Praktek (internship) project for the Momoyo Franchise, Cianjur Region, covering 2 branches (Ciranjang & Sukaluyu).

**Key features:**
- Role management: Owner, Manager, and Branch Head with distinct permissions
- KPI system with 3 indicators: Attendance (auto-calculated), Discipline, and Sales Contribution
- Automatic salary formula: Base Salary + Overtime Pay + Bonus − Deductions
- Payroll & performance reporting module with PDF export (DomPDF)
- Custom-designed login page with animation
